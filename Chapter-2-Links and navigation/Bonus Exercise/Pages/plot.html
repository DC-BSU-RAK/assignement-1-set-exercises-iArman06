Plot (season-wise)

Season 1:

The 100 teenages land on planet earth and soon realize that they are not alone. They face deadly animals, harsh environment and a group know as the Grounders.


Season 2:

The Group discovers Mount Weaher which is basically a facility housing another group of survivors, but the main aim of the mount weather people was to extract the bone marrow from the survivors.

Season 3:

A powerful AI named A.L.I.E who was responsible for the nuclear disaster returns, and it helps the survivors by by uploading the consciousness of the survivors to a virtual world.

Season 4:

Another nuclear apocalypse starts to happen on Earth. The survivors race to find a shelter to save themselves from the upcoming apocalypse as the world becomes unhabitable again.

Season 5:

6 years later after the 2nd apocalypse, survivors returns from space and also from the underground bunkers. New enemies arrive and all of them fight fro the last habitable land on earth.

Season 6:

Anew planet named Sanctum was discovered. But it holds secrets including body snatching rulers so they can live forever. The group of survivors fights among a new group of society.

Season 7:

This season explore a anomaly and the concept of transcendence. The series ends with most characters choosing a peaceful life on a new Earth like Planet after passing a final test of moral unity and growth.
