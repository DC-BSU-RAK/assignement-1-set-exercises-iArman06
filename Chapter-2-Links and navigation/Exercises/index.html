Overview

A century after Earth was devastated by a nuclear apocalypse, 100 space station residents are sent to the planet to determine whether it's habitable.

The 100 is an american apocalyptic based drama series that was displayed on The CW from March 19, 2014 to September 30, 2020. Developed by Jason Rothenberg, the show is completely based on the young adult nover series written by Kass Morgan.

The story begin 97 years after a nuclear apocalypse which erased almost all forms of life from planet earth. The only know survivors are those who escaped the earth into space and because of that incident, now they live on a massive space station called the Ark, which was formed from the combination of various international space stations.

With the Ark's life system getting malfunctioned, the leaders of the Ark had to make a bold decision. The decision being sending 100 tenage prisoners back to earth to test the present condition of the planet Earth, whether the planet is again habitable or not.

As the teens land on earth, they must learn to survive the harsh condition of the environment and also themselves. Soon they discover that they are not alone and that Earth is already inhabited by various groups including the fierce group called Grounders. Along with the Grouders there were Mountain Men, the powerful and dengerious Praxians as well.

The Webseries covers the brutal story about survival, sacrifices, and the consequence of the decisions made by the leaders. Each season expands the universe including more and more dilemmas and characters to confront what it really means to be "the good guys".

Plot (season-wise)

Season 1:

The 100 teenages land on planet earth and soon realize that they are not alone. They face deadly animals, harsh environment and a group know as the Grounders.


Season 2:

The Group discovers Mount Weaher which is basically a facility housing another group of survivors, but the main aim of the mount weather people was to extract the bone marrow from the survivors.

Season 3:

A powerful AI named A.L.I.E who was responsible for the nuclear disaster returns, and it helps the survivors by by uploading the consciousness of the survivors to a virtual world.

Season 4:

Another nuclear apocalypse starts to happen on Earth. The survivors race to find a shelter to save themselves from the upcoming apocalypse as the world becomes unhabitable again.

Season 5:

6 years later after the 2nd apocalypse, survivors returns from space and also from the underground bunkers. New enemies arrive and all of them fight fro the last habitable land on earth.

Season 6:

Anew planet named Sanctum was discovered. But it holds secrets including body snatching rulers so they can live forever. The group of survivors fights among a new group of society.

Season 7:

This season explore a anomaly and the concept of transcendence. The series ends with most characters choosing a peaceful life on a new Earth like Planet after passing a final test of moral unity and growth.
